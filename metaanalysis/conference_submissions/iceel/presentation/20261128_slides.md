# Background

## S3 ｜ Key Words: The Big Five

### The Big Five = 5 Personality Traits

| Trait | In simple words |
|---|---|
| Openness | Interest in new things |
| **Conscientiousness** | Careful and well-planned |
| **Extraversion** | Outgoing |
| Agreeableness | Kind and cooperative |

Face-to-face classes: **Conscientiousness** has the strongest link with grades.

# Results

## S9 ｜ Result 1: Extraversion

### Result 1: Extraversion Goes the Opposite Way

| | China | Japan |
|---|---|---|
| Individualism | 43 | **62** |
| Extraversion × grades (r) | −0.074 | **−0.173** |

Expected: Extraversion helps more in individualistic cultures.

⚠ Only a two-country comparison

フッタ：Yu (2021); Rivers (2021). China r converted from β (Peterson & Brown 2005)

# Discussion

## S12 ｜ Why Is Neuroticism Positive?

### Why Is Neuroticism Positive? (A Hypothesis)

**Japan: very high Uncertainty Avoidance (92)**

Hofstede (1986): in such cultures, students
- like **structured learning**
- are **rewarded for accuracy**

→ Students who worry a little may **study more carefully**

⚠ **Only a hypothesis**
- Both values are small and **not significant**
- Culture scores = **countries**; correlations = **individual students**

フッタ：Hofstede (1986) *International Journal of Intercultural Relations*

> メモ：上半分（説明）と下半分（⚠ 注意）を線で分ける。下半分の2点は質疑で必ず聞かれる点なので、文字を小さくしない。
