## S9 ｜ Key Word: pooled r

### Key Word: pooled r

**pooled r = one average of r values from many studies**

**Bigger studies count more.**

pooled r = 0.002
→ "When we put all 12 studies together, the link is almost zero."

## S12 ｜ Key Word: Moderator

### Key Word: Moderator

**A moderator = a condition that changes the strength or direction of a link**

Example: Umbrella → staying dry
The link is strong **only on rainy days**
→ **Weather** is the moderator

**Today's question:**
Is **course format** a moderator of the link between personality and grades?

# Method

### Splitting 12 Studies by Format

| Format | Studies |
|---|---|
| 🔵 Asynchronous | **6** |
| 🔴 Mixed-online | **5** |

> メモ：研究単位の本数（6/5/1）。特性ごとの研究数はこれより少ない（Extraversion：非同期 3、ミックス 5）。S16 の表に k を載せる。

---

## S14 ｜ Two Steps of Analysis

### Two Steps of Analysis

**Step 1 — Pool by format**
For each trait × format: combine r values (bigger studies count more)
Random-effects model → **10 pooled r values** (5 traits × 2 formats)

**Step 2 — One regression model**
42 rows (each study × each trait)
r is explained by: **trait** + **format** + **trait × format**
→ Is the **combination** significant?

**If both steps give the same answer, we can trust it more.**

> メモ：左右2段組（Step 1 ／ Step 2）にすると見やすい。Step 2 の「trait × format」を強調色で。

---

## S15 ｜ Key Words: Confidence Interval, p, Q between

### Key Words for the Results

**Confidence interval** — the true value is probably inside this range

**p value** — the chance of this result by luck alone
　**p < 0.05** → probably not just luck

**Q between** — how far apart the two pooled r values are (async vs. mixed)
　Standard **test for subgroup differences**
　With 2 groups: **Q > 3.84 → p < 0.05**

Sources: Borenstein et al. (2009), *Introduction to Meta-Analysis*; Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions.

> メモ：3つを縦に並べ、各キーワードを太字・大きめに。

# Discussion

## S20 ｜ Why? And What Course Designers Can Do

### What Course Designers Can Do

**Why minus in asynchronous courses?** *(hypothesis)*
Extraverted students get energy from people.
Studying alone for a long time may lower motivation.

**For Conscientiousness:** support planning and self-control — works in every format
**For Extraversion:** if the course is only asynchronous, **add a few live sessions**

**Not about changing personality. It is about course design.**

> メモ：「hypothesis」をはっきり表示する（論文で示したことではない）。

## S21 ｜ Limitations

### Limitations

1. **Small number of studies** (Extraversion: async **3**, mixed 5)
　→ **A discovery, not a final answer**
2. **Format coding**: 4 studies coded by the author
3. **Only 1 synchronous study**: nothing can be said about live-only classes
