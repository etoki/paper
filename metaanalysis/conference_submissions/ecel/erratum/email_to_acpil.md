## English

**To:** helen@academic-conferences.org
**Cc:** info@academic-conferences.org
**Subject:** ECEL 2026 – Paper EL-095 (Tokiwa): request to publish a correction

Dear Helen,

I am the author of paper EL-095, "Modality matters for Extraversion: A Big Five meta-regression in online learning environments", which I presented at ECEL 2026 and which is published in the conference proceedings.

After publication, I found errors in the statistical analysis of this paper, and I would like to ask for a correction to be published. In brief:

1. The modality moderator test in Table 2 used an inappropriate procedure. With the correct procedure, the Extraversion result is p = .011 rather than p < .001.
2. The interaction model in Table 3 treated non-independent observations as independent, so its p-values are not valid. I would like to withdraw this table.
3. Some values in Table 1 were incorrect, including two rows carried over from an earlier draft.
4. The Extraversion result depends on two studies from East Asia, so it is less robust than the paper states.

The finding for Conscientiousness does not change. The Extraversion finding is weaker and should be presented as a hypothesis rather than an established effect.

I attach a correction notice (Word file) with the corrected tables and text. Could you please let me know how you prefer to handle this? For example, you could publish the attached notice linked to the paper, or replace the published PDF with a corrected version and a short correction note. I am happy to revise the notice, or to provide a corrected version of the full paper in any format you need.

I apologise for these errors and for the extra work this causes.

Kind regards,
