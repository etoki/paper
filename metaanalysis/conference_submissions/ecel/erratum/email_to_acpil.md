## English

**To:** helen@academic-conferences.org
**Cc:** info@academic-conferences.org
**Subject:** ECEL 2026 – Paper EL-095 (Tokiwa): request to correct the published paper

Dear Helen,

I am the author of paper EL-095, "Modality matters for Extraversion: A Big Five meta-regression in online learning environments", which I presented at ECEL 2026 and which is published in the conference proceedings.

After publication, I found errors in the statistical analysis of this paper. In brief:

1. The modality moderator test in Table 2 used an inappropriate procedure. With the correct procedure, the Extraversion result is p = .011 rather than p < .001.
2. The interaction model in Table 3 treated non-independent observations as independent, so its p-values are not valid. This table needs to be withdrawn.
3. Some values in Table 1 were incorrect, including two rows carried over from an earlier draft.
4. The Extraversion result depends on two studies from East Asia, so it is less robust than the paper states.

The finding for Conscientiousness does not change. The Extraversion finding is weaker and should be presented as a hypothesis rather than an established effect. The title of the paper would stay the same.

Would it be possible to replace the published paper with a corrected version, together with a short note that gives the date and the content of the correction? If a replacement is not possible, I can instead provide a separate correction notice to be published and linked to the paper.

If you agree, I will send the corrected paper and a full list of the changes in whatever format you need.

I apologise for these errors and for the extra work this causes.

Kind regards,
